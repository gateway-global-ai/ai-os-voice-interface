# AMD-2026-010 — Vibe Coding University Identity Alignment, Selective Admission, and Value-First Funnel

## 0. Status and Intent

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Status** | `draft` with founder author validation recorded — ratification remains a separate governance state. |
| **Intent** | Convert the Vibe Coding University public identity and onboarding posture into governance: audience clarity, purpose-led admissions, value-first education, and bounded shell/user journey alignment. |
| **Non-goal** | This amendment does not set final pricing, accept any applicant automatically, or replace commercial/legal terms. |

This amendment treats the public landing page as a constitutional surface, not merely marketing copy. The first contact with Vibe Coding University SHALL express the institution's role, audience, and admission standard with enough clarity that downstream shell states, sales flows, curriculum, and agent behavior remain aligned.

## 1. Purpose

1. Establish Vibe Coding University as a **purpose-led, value-first, selective education platform**.
2. Prevent public identity drift where the platform appears to be a low-cost content club, generic community, or open enrollment product.
3. Align the home page, sales funnel, shell menu, university panel, registration flow, and agent prompts around the same constitutional identity.
4. Make the three primary audiences explicit:
   - **Students**
   - **Professors**
   - **Guest Speakers**
5. Preserve the governing principle **People Over Profits** while allowing premium pricing when it reflects high-value education, coaching, tools, resources, and selective access.

## 2. Normative Identity Standard

Vibe Coding University SHALL present itself as:

- a place for serious builders and learners;
- a community that provides value before asking for commitment;
- an educational platform for people driven by purpose, agency, and future-building;
- a selective university-style program, not an automatic-access subscription club;
- a platform that welcomes free community participation while reserving full curriculum participation for accepted applicants.

The public-facing identity SHALL NOT imply that money alone grants full university access. Payment may be part of participation, but admission, acceptance, fit, and purpose alignment are distinct gates.

## 3. People Over Profits

The phrase **People Over Profits** SHALL be interpreted as a binding platform posture:

1. The platform exists to create human value before extracting economic value.
2. Free community participation and free educational access MAY be offered to reduce friction and build trust.
3. Premium pricing MAY be used for full curriculum participation when it funds coaching, expert guidance, serious curriculum delivery, tools, and resources.
4. Pricing SHALL NOT be positioned as a bargain-bin club or casual entertainment product.
5. The full curriculum MAY be positioned as a premium commitment, including potential pricing around high-value monthly participation, but final prices SHALL be controlled by commercial/legal artifacts and active offer configuration.

## 4. Audience Funnel

The public home page SHALL expose a first-step menu for the three audience paths.

### 4.1 Students

Students are users who are serious about AI, are building a future around it, and may be leaving or reshaping high-paying careers because they are driven by purpose.

The student path SHALL offer:

- community account creation or login;
- free community access where applicable;
- a request/application path for full University consideration;
- messaging that full participation requires application and acceptance.

### 4.2 Professors

Professors are entrepreneurs, developers, educators, or practitioners with real success or expertise who can educate and inspire the next generation of builders.

The professor path SHALL offer:

- community account creation or login;
- a discovery/request path to be considered for the platform;
- messaging that compensation may be paid to professors;
- messaging that the platform seeks a high level of expertise and value contribution.

### 4.3 Guest Speakers

Guest speakers are people with stories, expertise, perspective, or insight that the vibe coding community should hear.

The guest speaker path SHALL offer:

- community account creation or login;
- a request path for podcast, event, or video meeting participation;
- messaging tied to topics such as platform economics, developer assistance, AI systems, entrepreneurship, and purpose-led building.

## 5. Shell and Intent Alignment

The AI OS shell SHALL map the audience path into shell state and skills:

```text
audience selection
  -> onboarding intent
  -> shell state
  -> available skills
  -> allowed actions
  -> evidence and follow-up
```

The shell SHALL NOT present all actions to all audiences. A student's onboarding path, a professor's discovery path, and a guest speaker's request path MAY share account creation and login mechanics, but their intent, copy, forms, follow-up, and review status SHALL remain distinguishable.

## 6. Admission and Access Boundary

Full University access SHALL be selective.

1. Account creation SHALL NOT equal admission.
2. Community membership SHALL NOT equal full curriculum access.
3. Payment SHALL NOT equal automatic acceptance unless a later ratified commercial/legal document explicitly defines a specific offer as open enrollment.
4. The shell and Supabase state SHOULD distinguish:
   - `community_member`
   - `student_applicant`
   - `accepted_student`
   - `professor_applicant`
   - `approved_professor`
   - `guest_speaker_applicant`
   - `approved_guest_speaker`
5. Any UI or agent output that collapses these states into a single "joined" status SHALL be treated as identity drift.

## 7. Leadership and Non-Dependency Doctrine

Vibe Coding University SHALL train leadership, not dependency.

AI is a powerful accelerator, but it is not a substitute for judgment, education, purpose, or leadership. The platform SHALL NOT teach students to surrender agency to an LLM, follow AI outputs uncritically, or mistake generated code for mastery.

The governing doctrine is:

1. Leaders lead. AI assists.
2. A human with a clear purpose, strong judgment, and well-developed ideas can produce direction that exceeds generic AI output.
3. AI outputs SHALL be treated as inputs to disciplined human judgment, not as the final source of authority.
4. Students SHALL be trained to understand systems, tools, platform economics, and execution boundaries before relying on automation.
5. The platform SHALL reject dependency loops where users outsource thinking, standards, and accountability to an LLM.
6. The University SHALL cultivate builders who can exercise control over what influences them, including AI systems, agents, prompts, tools, and social pressure.

This doctrine is directly tied to the AI OS model. Shell state, skill authority, and bounded tools exist because unrestricted AI access creates dependency, confusion, and invisible authority transfer. The University exists to teach the opposite: disciplined agency.

## 8. Resonance and Identity Synchronization

The platform SHALL avoid invisible friction caused by identity mismatch. A public surface that looks like a casual low-cost product while the underlying institution seeks serious, purpose-driven builders creates misalignment.

AI OS SHALL treat identity synchronization as a design and governance concern:

- audience;
- visual hierarchy;
- copy;
- onboarding path;
- shell state;
- skill authority;
- admissions policy;
- pricing posture;
- community promise.

These elements SHOULD reinforce the same identity. Where they conflict, the platform SHALL prefer clarity, purpose, and trust over short-term conversion pressure.

## 9. Platform Economics

This amendment recognizes that clear identity improves platform economics:

- fewer unqualified leads;
- less support time spent explaining fit;
- stronger attraction of professors and speakers;
- better conversion from aligned students;
- lower tool and agent waste caused by ambiguous intent;
- stronger brand trust because value precedes extraction.

The platform SHOULD optimize for the right people, not the most people.

## 11. Author Approval and Ratification Boundary

The founder's approval recorded in this amendment is a valid author validation signal. It establishes that the alignment doctrine, People Over Profits posture, leadership/non-dependency doctrine, and three-audience funnel represent the intended institutional identity.

This SHALL NOT be confused with automated ratification. If governance process later requires formal peer review or council ratification for constitutional merge, that process may still run. However, implementation teams SHALL NOT treat lack of AI triangulation as a reason to weaken the doctrine, soften the audience standard, or reposition the University as a generic low-cost AI club.

## 12. No-Mock Gate

This amendment SHALL NOT be marked ratified from intention alone.

Acceptance requires:

- a real public landing page implementation;
- source-backed menu/action mapping;
- Supabase or form-state plan for audience-specific requests;
- no false claim of admission or payment configuration;
- reviewer notes confirming People Over Profits, selective admission, and audience clarity remain intact.

If any evidence is missing, status remains `draft`, `review_pending`, `blocked`, or `unverified`.
